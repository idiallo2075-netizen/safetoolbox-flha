## What Is a Field Level Hazard Assessment (FLHA)?

A Field Level Hazard Assessment (FLHA) is a job-site safety process used to identify hazards associated with a specific task, evaluate potential risks, and determine appropriate control measures before work begins.

An FLHA may need to be reviewed and updated when site conditions, equipment, or work activities change.

## Common Construction Hazards

Field level hazard assessments can address a variety of construction safety risks.

### Working at Heights
Identify fall hazards, access conditions, and appropriate protective measures.

### Heavy Equipment and Moving Vehicles
Review equipment movement, visibility, pedestrian interactions, and work-zone controls.

### Excavation and Ground Conditions
Assess ground stability, underground utilities, access, and other task-specific excavation hazards.

### Weather and Environmental Conditions
Consider extreme temperatures, precipitation, wind, icy surfaces, and changing site conditions.

### Tools and Equipment
Review equipment condition, safe operating procedures, and task-specific protective measures.

These examples are general educational information and do not replace a site-specific hazard assessment.

## Why Are Field Level Hazard Assessments Important?

FLHAs support workers and contractors by helping them:

- Identify hazards before beginning work.
- Document appropriate safety controls.
- Communicate hazards among crew members.
- Review changes in site conditions.
- Maintain organized safety documentation.
- Encourage workers to participate in hazard identification.

## Frequently Asked Questions

### What does FLHA stand for?

FLHA stands for **Field Level Hazard Assessment**. It is commonly used in Canadian construction and other industries to identify task-specific hazards and control measures.

### When should a Field Level Hazard Assessment be completed?

An FLHA is commonly completed before starting a work activity and should be reviewed when work conditions, tasks, equipment, or identified hazards change. Applicable employer procedures and occupational health and safety requirements must also be followed.

### Is an FLHA the same as a Job Hazard Analysis (JHA)?

Both FLHA and JHA processes involve identifying work-related hazards and determining controls. However, their format, scope, and application may differ depending on the employer, industry, and jurisdiction.

### Can small contractors use digital FLHA tools?

Yes. Digital documentation can help small contractors organize hazard assessment records, provided the assessments are appropriate for the work, reviewed by responsible personnel, and meet applicable requirements.

### Does an FLHA eliminate workplace hazards?

No. An FLHA supports hazard identification and risk control planning. Actual safety depends on implementing effective controls, following applicable requirements, and reviewing changing job-site conditions.
